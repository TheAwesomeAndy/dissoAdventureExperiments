## Notes

- **Attribution.** For code, retain the MIT notice. For text and original
  figures, cite: Lane, A. A. (2026). *Affective Reservoir-Spike Processing and
  Inference Network (ARSPI-Net): A Four-Level Interpretable Neuromorphic
  Framework for Clinical EEG Analysis.* PhD Dissertation, Stony Brook
  University. See [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff).
- **Derived numerical outputs** (e.g. `results_manifest.json`) are code
  outputs and fall under MIT, but they describe results computed from the
  restricted SHAPE data; they contain summary statistics only, no
  participant-level data.
- **Third-party figures** were included under academic fair use for a
  dissertation. If you are unsure whether a given figure in `pictures/` is
  original or third-party, treat it as third-party and check the corresponding
  chapter's citation before reuse.
- If any third-party material is identified that cannot be included even under
  fair use, open an issue and it will be removed or replaced.
